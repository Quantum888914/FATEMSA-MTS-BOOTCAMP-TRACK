Hardware Engineering & IoT Track

From fundamental circuit analysis and firmware architecture to edge telemetry and cloud dashboards.

Tutor: Adam Adekanye
Schedule

Sat & Sun (60-90 Mins / Day)

Total Duration

6 Weeks (12 Sessions)

Lab Platforms

ESP32, Wokwi & Tinkercad

Final Milestone

Smart Connected Prototype

Week 01 • Nov 21–22 Electronics Foundations & Embedded Prototyping
▼
Day 1: Circuit Principles (75m)
  • Voltage, Current, Resistance, & Ohm's Law in practice.
  • Component essentials: Resistors, LEDs, Switches, Breadboards.
  • Microcontroller vs. Microprocessor architecture fundamentals.
  • Power rails, ground references, and short-circuit prevention.
Day 2: Hands-on Lab (75m)
  • Setting up the IDE (Arduino IDE / Wokwi Simulator workspace).
  • ESP32 Pinout: GPIOs, 3.3V vs 5V logic safety.
  • Wiring an LED circuit with calculated current-limiting resistors.
  • Live Lab: Manual push-button LED logic circuit.
Milestone Assignment 1

Replicate a 3-stage traffic signal circuit on Wokwi/Tinkercad with proper resistor calculations and manual button input.

Week 02 • Nov 28–29 Firmware Logic, Digital I/O & Non-Blocking Timing
▼
Day 3: Digital Inputs & Outputs (75m)
  • Firmware lifecycle: Execution flow of setup() and loop().
  • Digital writes: Active-high vs active-low logic levels.
  • Digital reads: Floating pins and internal pull-up configuration.
  • Contact bounce phenomenon & hardware/software debouncing.
Day 4: Control Logic & Timing (75m)
  • The hazards of delay() in responsive embedded firmware.
  • Non-blocking timing patterns using millis() counters.
  • Building state-driven control logic for user interfaces.
  • Live Lab: Multi-function button controlling alarm modes.
Milestone Assignment 2

Program a non-blocking dual-mode security beacon (Idle state vs Alert state triggered by push-button).

Week 03 • Dec 05–06 Analog Signals, ADC & Environmental Sensing
▼
Day 5: ADC & Voltage Dividers (75m)
  • Analog vs Digital signals: Resolution, voltage reference, and ADC steps.
  • Resistive sensing: Reading Photoresistors (LDR) & Thermistors.
  • Voltage divider equations & signal conditioning circuits.
  • Pulse Width Modulation (PWM) for analog output simulation.
Day 6: Digital Sensors (75m)
  • Interfacing modular digital sensors (DHT11/22 or Ultrasonic HC-SR04).
  • Sensor data calibration, noise reduction & moving average filters.
  • Threshold trigger logic for automated physical responses.
  • Live Lab: Smart ambient light-dimmer & obstacle detector.
Milestone Assignment 3

Build an automated temperature and proximity alert monitor that sounds a piezo buzzer when limits are breached.

Week 04 • Dec 12–13 Serial Communication Protocols & Local Displays
▼
Day 7: Bus Architectures (75m)
  • Serial protocols overview: UART vs I2C vs SPI protocols.
  • UART debugging: Baud rates, buffers & structured telemetry.
  • I2C 2-wire bus architecture: Master-Slave addressing, SDA & SCL.
  • Troubleshooting bus collisions & missing pull-up resistors.
Day 8: Display UI Interfacing (75m)
  • Driving I2C OLED screens (SSD1306) or 16x2 LCD modules.
  • Formatting on-screen telemetry, live gauges & status icons.
  • Integrating multiple I2C peripherals on a shared two-wire bus.
  • Live Lab: Multi-sensor environmental dashboard on OLED.
Milestone Assignment 4

Design a compact local telemetry station displaying temperature, humidity, and system uptime on an OLED screen.

Week 05 • Dec 19–20 Internet of Things (IoT) & Cloud Data Telemetry
▼
Day 9: Wi-Fi & Protocol Theory (75m)
  • IoT Architecture: Edge nodes, Gateways, Cloud Brokers, Clients.
  • ESP32 Wi-Fi Station (STA) mode & automatic connection handlers.
  • Transport layer comparison: Lightweight MQTT vs HTTP REST APIs.
  • MQTT Publish/Subscribe model, topics & JSON payloads.
Day 10: Cloud Dashboards (75m)
  • Connecting to cloud platforms (Adafruit IO / Blynk / HiveMQ broker).
  • Streaming live edge telemetry feeds into cloud charts.
  • Remote actuator control: Toggling relays/LEDs from cloud buttons.
  • Live Lab: Bidirectional IoT link with remote control & graph.
Milestone Assignment 5

Configure an IoT edge node that uploads periodic telemetry every 15s and accepts a remote override toggle.

Week 06 • Dec 26–27 System Hardening, Cross-Track Synthesis & Summit Pitch
▼
Day 11: Edge Reliability (75m)
  • Edge hardening: Watchdog timers, crash handling, power transients.
  • Battery selection, power budgeting & sleep modes overview.
  • Cross-track handoff: Formatted JSON APIs for Web & AI teams.
  • Wiring cleanliness, wire dressing & prototype packaging.
Day 12: Review & Demos (75m)
  • Live testing clinic, code optimization & sensor recalibration.
  • Demo staging: Crafting a 3-minute technical pitch & live demo.
  • Project rubric assessment against reliability & functionality.
  • Capstone Demo: End-to-end multi-track integrated showcase.
Final Deliverable

Complete smart prototype (Smart Agriculture, Asset Tracker, or Energy Meter) ready for Muslim Tech Summit presentation.

AI & Machine Learning Track

A practical, beginner-friendly pathway to data exploration, model training, evaluation metrics, and responsible AI.

Tutor: Abdullateef Atanda
Schedule

Sat & Sun (~2 Hours / Weekend)

Total Duration

6 Weeks

Lab Tools

Python, Google Colab, Scikit-learn

Final Milestone

Trained ML Model & Notebook Report

Week 01 • Nov 21–22 Introduction to AI, Machine Learning, and Python
▼
Saturday: Concepts & Guided Learning (60m)
  • What is Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning?
  • Supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning.
  • Real-world applications: finance, healthcare, agriculture, security.
  • The ML workflow: problem definition to prediction.
Sunday: Application & Practice (60m)
  • Introduction to Python & Google Colab environment.
  • Variables, data types, lists, dictionaries & operators.
  • Conditional statements, loops & simple functions.
  • Practical: Small Python program with input/output calculations.
Weekly Assignment 1

Identify one real-world problem AI/ML could solve. Explain the beneficiaries, dataset needed, and submit a basic Python Colab notebook.

Week 02 • Nov 28–29 Python for Data Analysis & Visualizations
▼
Saturday: NumPy & Pandas Foundations (60m)
  • Introduction to NumPy and Pandas dataframes.
  • Understanding rows, columns, features, and records.
  • Loading and inspecting sample CSV datasets.
  • Summary statistics and data distributions.
Sunday: Cleaning & Visualization (60m)
  • Handling missing values and duplicate records.
  • Basic data-cleaning techniques in Pandas.
  • Matplotlib: Bar charts, line charts, and histograms.
  • Practical: Clean a small dataset and generate 2 data plots.
Weekly Assignment 2

Explore a beginner dataset with Pandas, resolve at least one data-quality issue, and submit a notebook with 2 visualizations and 3 observations.

Week 03 • Dec 05–06 Understanding & Building Machine Learning Models
▼
Saturday: Model Theory & Algorithms (60m)
  • How machines learn: Features and target variables.
  • Classification vs. Regression problem framing.
  • Introduction to Linear/Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, KNN.
  • Choosing the right algorithm for tabular data.
Sunday: Scikit-learn Implementation (60m)
  • Feature matrix (X) and target vector (y) separation.
  • Splitting data: Train/test split methodology.
  • Training a baseline model using Scikit-learn.
  • Practical: Generate predictions on unseen test records.
Weekly Assignment 3

Train a basic machine learning model using a provided dataset. Submit your notebook explaining features, algorithm choice, and output.

Week 04 • Dec 12–13 Model Evaluation and Responsible AI
▼
Saturday: Performance Metrics (60m)
  • Accuracy limitations in imbalanced datasets.
  • Precision, Recall, F1-score & Confusion Matrices.
  • Mean Absolute Error (MAE) and RMSE for regression.
  • Understanding Underfitting vs. Overfitting.
Sunday: Ethics & Generative AI (60m)
  • Bias in datasets and machine learning predictions.
  • Data privacy, transparency, and model explainability.
  • Introduction to Generative AI & Large Language Models (LLMs).
  • Practical: Case study analysis on algorithmic bias.
Weekly Assignment 4

Evaluate your Week 3 model using precision/recall or MAE. Document one limitation and propose a data-level improvement.

Week 05 • Dec 19–20 Final Project Development & Pipeline Build
▼
Saturday: Project Scoping & Sourcing (60m)
  • Defining project objectives and success criteria.
  • Sourcing open datasets (Kaggle / UCI Repository).
  • Feature selection, preprocessing, and pipeline design.
  • Tutor feedback clinic on proposed topics.
Sunday: Guided Implementation (60m)
  • Building an end-to-end ML pipeline in Google Colab.
  • Model tuning and iterative evaluation.
  • Error debugging and testing with hypothetical samples.
  • Practical: Complete first working model prototype.
Weekly Assignment 5

Complete working project prototype notebook with short draft explaining problem, dataset, method, and results.

Week 06 • Dec 26–27 Project Completion, Review & Presentation
▼
Saturday: Polishing & Slide Prep (60m)
  • Finalizing documentation, comments, and markdown cells.
  • Preparing presentation slides or notebook walkthrough.
  • Reviewing project grading rubric with mentors.
Sunday: Live Project Demos (60m)
  • Live cohort presentations (3 mins per participant/team).
  • Explaining problem, dataset, algorithm, and real-world impact.
  • Tutor assessment and selection of Top 5 Summit contenders.
Final Deliverable

Submitted project notebook, short report, and presentation slides ready for the Muslim Tech Summit exhibition showcase.

Product Design (3D CAD) Track

Parametric part modeling, multi-component assemblies, technical documentation, and design for 3D printing.

In Partnership with Lagos SWUG
Program Plan

6 Sessions × 5 Days = 30 Days

Daily Commitment

1 Hour / Day (30 Total Hours)

Software

SOLIDWORKS

Final Project

Fabrication-Ready 3D Prototype

Session 01 • Days 1–5 (5 Hours) Introduction to 3D CAD & Parametric Modelling
▼
Curriculum Topics
  • Introduction to CAD, SolidWorks UI and viewport navigation.
  • 2D sketches, geometric relations, and sketch dimensions.
  • Fully defined sketches and preserving design intent.
  • Extrude boss/base, extruded cut, and revolved features.
Hands-on Practice
  • Daily guided drafting of standard prismatic geometry.
  • Troubleshooting over-defined and dangling sketch entities.
  • Mini-Project: Model a simple mechanical spacer/component.
Session Milestone

Portfolio Model 1: Fully defined 3D mechanical component submitted in native SLDPRT format.

Session 02 • Days 6–10 (5 Hours) Advanced Part Modelling & Design Tables
▼
Curriculum Topics
  • Revolve, swept boss/base, and lofted solids.
  • Linear/circular patterns, mirror features, and design reuse.
  • Fillets, chamfers, shells, and structural ribs.
  • Configurations and Excel-driven design tables.
Hands-on Practice
  • Modeling organic curved profiles and structural brackets.
  • Hollowing out geometry with uniform wall thicknesses.
  • Mini-Project: Model a functional mechanical casing.
Session Milestone

Portfolio Model 2: Functional mechanical casing with ribs, shell, and mounting bosses.

Session 03 • Days 11–15 (5 Hours) Engineering Drawings & Design Documentation
▼
Curriculum Topics
  • Drawing environment, standard sheet formats, and title blocks.
  • Orthographic projections, isometric, and detailed section views.
  • Dimensions, manufacturing tolerances, and surface annotations.
  • Exploded views and automated Bill of Materials (BOM).
Hands-on Practice
  • Exporting 2D mechanical engineering blueprints.
  • Assigning part numbers, material specs, and drawing revisions.
  • Mini-Project: Complete manufacturing drawing package.
Session Milestone

Portfolio Package 3: Production drawing sheet PDF complete with title block, GD&T, and section details.

Session 04 • Days 16–20 (5 Hours) Assembly Design & Component Packaging
▼
Curriculum Topics
  • Assembly workspace, importing parts, and ground components.
  • Standard mates: Coincident, concentric, parallel, and distance.
  • Subassemblies, flexible assemblies, and motion degrees.
  • Clearance verification, interference detection, and collision checks.
Hands-on Practice
  • Kinematic assembly mates for moving linkages.
  • Packaging electronic components within custom enclosures.
  • Mini-Project: Assemble a small mechanical system.
Session Milestone

Portfolio Model 4: Kinematic multi-part assembly file (SLDASM) with zero geometric interference.

Session 05 • Days 21–25 (5 Hours) Design for Physical Prototyping & Additive Manufacturing
▼
Curriculum Topics
  • Design for Additive Manufacturing (DFAM) fundamentals.
  • Minimum wall thickness, overhang angles, clearances, and print orientation.
  • Exporting high-resolution STL and 3MF mesh formats.
  • Introduction to slicers (Cura/PrusaSlicer) and infill patterns.
Hands-on Practice
  • Optimizing part geometry for rapid FDM 3D printing.
  • Calculating print volume, material usage, and layer heights.
  • Mini-Project: Prototype-ready hardware enclosure.
Session Milestone

Portfolio Deliverable 5: Validated STL/3MF model file sliced and certified print-ready.

Session 06 • Days 26–30 (5 Hours) Capstone Design & Summit Showcase
▼
Capstone Workflow
  • Define engineering problem statement & requirements.
  • Model individual parametric parts and custom packaging.
  • Assemble system, verify kinematic mates, and eliminate collisions.
  • Produce production drawings and rendered 3D visuals.
Suggested Capstone Projects
  • Mini Desk Fan: Blades, hub, housing & motor mount.
  • Mini Gearbox: Spur gears, shafts, bearings & housing.
  • Adjustable Phone Stand: Parametric links & stability.
  • Mechanical Gripper: 4-bar linkage & motor bracket.
Final Deliverable

Complete mechanical CAD portfolio, rendering sheet, and 3D printing manufacturing files ready for MTS presentation.

Assignment Submission Workflow

Keep your track progress verified and qualify for completion certificates:

  • Step 1: Submit all weekly milestones through the central FATEMSA Google Form.
  • Hardware: Submit public Wokwi simulator URLs (or GitHub code + 30s video demo for physical circuits).
  • AI/ML: Submit public Google Colab links with executed output cells.
  • Product Design: Upload native SLDPRT/SLDASM files, PDF drawings, and exported STL meshes.
  • Web: Submit CodePen/StackBlitz links (W1–W3) and GitHub repos + live Vercel URLs (W4–W6).

Interdisciplinary Track Synergy

The 4 tracks are designed to collaborate directly on Muslim Tech Summit hackathon projects:

  • Hardware + Web: Hardware sensor nodes stream JSON data; Web track develops the cloud dashboard.
  • Hardware + Product Design: Design track creates custom parametric 3D printed enclosures for sensor boards.
  • Hardware + AI/ML: IoT sensors log physical telemetry to train predictive and anomaly detection models.
  • AI/ML + Web: AI models are served via APIs into interactive web interfaces for end users.
